The Journey & Philosophy

history_edu Origins

My fascination with visual storytelling began in the darkroom, developing 35mm film rolls by hand. That tactile connection to the medium taught me to value every single frame. Transitioning into the digital cinema era, I carried this discipline with me—treating high-resolution sensors like celluloid canvas.

Over the last decade, I've directed commercial spots for Fortune 500 brands and intimate documentaries in remote corners of the globe. Regardless of the scale, the core mission remains the same: to find the human truth buried under the noise.

lightbulb Approach

"Light is not just illumination; it is the brush with which we paint the subtext of a scene."

I prioritize narrative over spectacle. While I utilize cutting-edge technology—from heavy-lift drones to large-format cinema cameras—every movement is motivated by emotion. My visual philosophy is grounded in contrast: the interplay of shadow and light that mirrors the internal conflict of the characters I film.
